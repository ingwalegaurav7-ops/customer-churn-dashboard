Audience: Subscription retention / customer success team.
Decision: Prioritize high-churn groups for retention outreach.
Supporting metrics: churn rate, revenue at risk, tenure, contract, and payment method.
Uncertainty: This is a small fictional sample (15 customers); the dashboard is descriptive, not a causal or predictive model.
Next measurement: Recalculate these KPIs on a larger rolling cohort and track post-outreach churn, renewal, and retained monthly revenue.

KPI definitions: churn rate = churned customers ÷ filtered customers; retention rate = 1 − churn rate; revenue at risk = sum of monthly charges for churned customers; tenure KPI = average completed tenure months.
